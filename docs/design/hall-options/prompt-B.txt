VARIANT B — title exactly "B 曜黑主机"; subtitle "聚焦当前游戏 · 夜间舒适 · 手柄友好".
A dark console menu with deliberate visual calm, NOT cyberpunk and NOT neon. Use charcoal #181C22 canvas, deep slate #252C35 panels, off-white #F0F3F6 text, soft grey #9AA6B5 metadata, ice-cyan #53D8E5 focus / buttons, and very limited amber #DFA64F status. Presentation board also charcoal, captions in soft light grey.
Main HOME layout is structurally distinct from A: the selected cover is the largest element, about one third of the content width at left-center; its title 远野回声, installed chip, recent-play line and one 继续游戏 button sit to its RIGHT, forming a compact horizontal selected-game stage. Below this stage is one slim shelf of the other three covers, with thin focus details. Never use a full-bleed illustration as the background. The four navigation destinations live in a low, compact bottom-center capsule with simple circular icons. Keep generous dark negative space, thin low-contrast separators and high-contrast readable actions. The details, downloads, settings and saves follow this same disciplined dark visual system. No glows except a restrained 2px cyan focus edge, no glass blur, no black-on-black illegible labels. Memorable signature is the left cover plus right action panel, emphasizing immediate return to the current game.
