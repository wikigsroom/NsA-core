The presentation board is nearly white with refined grey caption text.
Main HOME layout: a spacious horizontal shelf of four large nearly-square cover tiles, title ABOVE the selected first tile, selected cover with a clean cyan/white double focus outline; a simple text-and-button strip below it, never an advertisement banner. Lower center has a broad very light grey rounded capsule holding four round, custom colored navigation icons with Chinese labels; selected 我的游戏 gets the focus ring and label. Avatar top left, clock top right, ample breathing room. Very subtle 12px-style corners, fine separators, no oversized bubbly cards. Settings are bright, flat, calm and genuinely console-like. The selected cover is the memorable element, everything else quietly supports it. Clean, tactile, approachable, ready for an Android touch interface.
